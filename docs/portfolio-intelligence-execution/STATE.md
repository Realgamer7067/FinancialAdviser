One typed table holds both risk assessments and scenario runs (spec lists them separately; this is a deliberate simplification): identity is `(user, kind, state_id, valuation_id, method_version, inputs_hash)`, get-or-create, replay by id, never re-priced.

Asset mix (direct equity share plus an `equity_share_upper_bound` that includes fund/ETF holdings because their contents are not looked through), issuer concentration over the identified direct-equity slice with its coverage (largest issuer, top 5, Herfindahl, effective positions), account concentration, sector (matched instrument master only; unmatched equity and funds stay in explicit unknown buckets), coverage/staleness, liquidity (unclaimed CASH only, after goal claims, vs 12 x essential outgo + known obligations; undefined when unknown or zero), and historical volatility/max drawdown. ## P4: "what changed" suggestions on Holdings and Watchlist (2026-10-01): **built, deployed, verified on fixtures, Postgres and live data**
