# Portfolio Intelligence Engine: implementation plan

Prepared 29 September 2026. This is the next product plan, not a claim that the described behavior exists today. Intended executor: one implementation agent, working serially, with no subagents. The existing [V3 plan](V3-IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md) and [execution ledger](v3-execution/STATE.md) remain historical records; this plan starts from the current code, not from V3's original assumptions.

> **Product rule:** Recommend a change only when it improves this person's financial position under their goals, existing holdings, risk capacity, constraints, costs, and uncertainty. A valid result is **hold cash or do nothing**.

## 1. Exact target and boundaries

The product's core is a versioned **Portfolio Twin**: financial profile + goals + accounts + normalized positions + commitments + valuations + evidence + policy. A deterministic **Portfolio Decision Engine (PDE)** evaluates candidate *actions against the whole twin*, including a no-action baseline. Research models produce evidence. They do not decide suitability or generate a stock score that is mistaken for personal advice.

The first complete release is a **local, single-user, read-only, India-focused portfolio review** for the owner's account. It imports Angel One holdings and supports manual and CSV assets from elsewhere; computes exposures, capacity inputs, basic stress tests, goal scenarios, and counterfactuals; presents a dated status and traceable reasoning. The first milestone is a usable private review, not a publicly accessible adviser. Automatic inbox/reviews follow after the core import → state → simulate journey works. It does not place or modify orders. It does not claim regulatory registration, live trading permission, guaranteed returns, tax advice, calibrated probabilities, or complete wealth coverage.

The immediate demo should work with one actual Angel account plus a fixture account when credentials are unavailable. Multi-user deployment, order execution, continuous tick feeds, CAS/OCR, fund look-through, tax-lot reconstruction, broad factor modeling, probabilistic goal claims, and fully automated alerts are later milestones with explicit gates below. Do not create empty adapters or routes for them just to match a diagram.

### The product insight to prove first

The memorable first experience is **“what looks diversified may not be.”** After import, show a sourced, dated account inventory; identify one concrete overlap or concentration *only when the data supports it*; let the user compare doing nothing with a change they choose; and explain whether the change improves the measured portfolio dimensions without breaking a personal constraint. If the imported portfolio is already well aligned, say so. This gives the project an identity without requiring a fake all-knowing portfolio-health number.

The work is ordered by dependency, not feature spectacle: **trustworthy holdings → trustworthy financial context → trustworthy risk observations → counterfactuals → decisions → ongoing memory.** A correlation graph, thesis decay animation or LLM council is useful only after the underlying identity, data coverage and decision accounting are sound. The initial demo should lead with a supported hidden-concentration finding, a goal or liquidity constraint, and a no-action or user-specified comparison; it should not require the system to discover a “best stock.”

### Priority filter for ideas from the product brief

| Build now because it changes the decision | Build after a measurable entry gate | Keep out of the first release |
|---|---|---|
| Angel import; account coexistence; risk capacity versus tolerance; concentration/coverage; dated stress illustration; HOLD baseline; why this/why not; decision audit | Dated fund look-through; correlation graph; thesis monitoring; event inbox; calibrated factor exposure; Monte Carlo goal probability; forecast ensemble | Order execution; autonomous multi-broker trading; full-universe five-level research on every review; five-role LLM voting as an investment signal; unsourced tax optimization |

The distinguishing IP is the **candidate-state comparison and refusal discipline**: every proposed action is applied to the same frozen financial state, independently checked against personal constraints, compared to HOLD, and rejected if its benefit is too small or unsupported. Research depth is allocated to questions that survive this cheap comparison. This is a concrete algorithmic advantage; it does not depend on inventing a proprietary numeric confidence score.

### Product success criteria

1. Onboarding reconstructs a usable state from Angel holdings or manual/CSV entries and clearly identifies missing accounts, missing cost basis, stale prices, and unresolved instruments.
2. The dashboard answers “Does anything require action?” before showing market noise. A no-action state is visible and normal.
3. A user can inspect current portfolio exposures, one stress scenario, and **do nothing** beside every feasible alternative; values reconcile to the same dated snapshot. If no other alternative is valid, show why instead of manufacturing three choices.
4. An action states its before/after metrics, binding constraints, materiality threshold, uncertainty, costs, and why the runner-up lost. No proposal is issued when a required input or independent risk gate fails.
5. Every published conclusion is reproducible by ID from frozen input versions. A later quote may update a live valuation panel, never rewrite a past decision.

Unknown identifiers remain unresolved and count toward `unknown_exposure`, not silently dropped. Multiple account snapshots combine the **latest complete, confirmed snapshot per account at a common review cutoff**; a new Angel sync must not replace manually entered assets. Account values with stale valuation retain their date and quality flag. Track whether an account is included, excluded, or stale by explicit user choice; never silently discard a disconnected account.

Financial profile stores actual/unknown income, essential expenses, debt payments, dependents, income stability, emergency reserve target, near-term obligations, liquidity needs, knowledge, risk answers, investment horizon per goal, prohibited assets/sectors, employer exposure, and one-time/monthly available budget. Preserve raw answers. Ask the user whether all relevant investment accounts/assets are represented; store a dated `complete/partial/unknown` coverage attestation and names/types of known missing accounts without pretending the broker can discover them. Until confirmed complete, headline value is **known portfolio value**, not total wealth. Monthly investable amount cannot exceed user-confirmed surplus by silent inference. Risk **capacity** uses cash buffer, surplus, debt, goal horizon and dependence on portfolio; risk **tolerance** uses behavior answers; risk **requirement** describes the return/risk tension to meet a goal. If capacity is lower, constrain by capacity and explain why. If inputs are missing, record `unknown` and restrict action types.

Record liabilities as dated, **positive outstanding balances** with payment, rate/terms and certainty where known. Asset value and liabilities remain separate; a debt is not a negative holding in asset-mix weights. “Known net financial position” is assets minus known liabilities and needs its own completeness label; do not call it net worth while homes, other assets or debts may be missing. A rate shock affects debt cash flow only when reset terms are known.

### Reconciliation and valuation rules

At import preview, calculate `sum(units × broker LTP)` where both are present and compare with Angel's `totalholdingvalue`; expose absolute/relative difference and source-time mismatch. Do not force equality if broker quantity or LTP semantics differ. Record raw broker aggregate separately. Select values by **asset-class-specific** policy: a fresh mapped exchange quote may supersede broker holding LTP for listed securities; a dated official NAV is needed for funds; a deposit uses confirmed principal/terms or user-reported value. Each selected value carries source, timestamp and stale label. Never label a weekend last close live. Broker P&L is source-reported, not reconstructed realized gain. Money is Decimal at storage/math boundaries; convert to float only inside statistics with explicit validation and rounded display.

Historical values require positions and cash flows at each date. The initial health timeline starts **from first state snapshot**; it does not invent prior portfolio performance from current holdings. Preserve fees, dividends, splits and transactions once available; without them, use “valuation snapshot change,” not “return.”

## 5. Core calculations and decision policy

### 5.1 Risk fingerprint: honest first release

Compute each dimension with an explicit formula, input window, coverage, and direction. Do not roll incomparable metrics into one magical 0–100 “health” score. Initial dimensions:

* Asset mix and equity share: weights over **known-valued** positions, including cash and an unknown **asset-class** bucket. Count unvalued positions separately; never assign them a zero weight. Keep separate unknown issuer, sector and look-through buckets so one missing classification does not erase classifications that are actually known.
* Single issuer concentration: largest verified issuer weight; top-five weight; Herfindahl index `Σw_i²`, effective positions `1/Σw_i²` **over the identified issuer slice**, with its portfolio coverage displayed. This is weight concentration, not true independent risk exposures; do not call it a whole-portfolio effective count when issuer identity is missing.
* Sector concentration: aggregate direct holdings; fund look-through only when sourced. Unknown remainder stays unknown.
* Liquidity coverage: confirmed accessible cash after earmarks / next 12 months essential outgo and known obligations **not already included** in that outgo. Show `undefined` when the denominator is zero or unknown. Broker RMS alone is insufficient.
* Volatility and drawdown: historical daily adjusted-return estimate only when ≥252 aligned observations and return-series coverage ≥80% of **known total portfolio value**; otherwise `insufficient_data`. Unvalued positions remain separately unquantified. Use a documented common-date window and minimum pairwise overlap. Historical max drawdown describes the observed window, not future worst case.
* Correlation clusters: ≥126 overlapping returns; shrink noisy correlations toward zero or a common factor; edge threshold (e.g. 0.70) is an explanatory setting, not a hard financial truth. Show cluster weight and sample count. Include unknown/fund exposure limitation.
* Staleness/coverage: fraction of value with fresh identity/valuation/history. This is separate from risk itself.

Later factor exposures use a documented regression against licensed/available factor series, with benchmark identity, window, standard errors, and out-of-sample stability. Until those inputs exist, label only directly observed sector, size/category or beta estimates; never print claims such as “67% driven by growth factor.” A proprietary score is earned through validation, not naming.

### 5.2 Stress and goals

First implement deterministic scenario vectors: broad equity −20%, banks −20%, IT −25%, rates +150bp where duration is known, oil +30% where exposure mapping is supported, and INR −10% where currency exposure is known. Shock mapping must be additive without applying broad-market and sector shocks twice; use an explicit residual/factor model or present separate scenarios. For each, `ΔV = Σ position_value × scenario_return`, plus any known cash/debt sensitivity. Unknown sensitivity produces a **coverage gap/range**, not zero effect. Label these “illustrations, not forecasts.” Show largest contributors and exact assumptions. Historical crisis templates require dated, sourced point-in-time return series and constituent coverage before showing 2008/2020 labels.

Reuse `cash_flow_engine.py` for monthly goal ledgers, earmarks, inflation basis, existing commitments and budget. Low/base/high assumptions are user/policy scenarios, not estimated success probabilities. Show gap and required monthly contribution under each. Add Monte Carlo **only** when return/covariance distributions, sequence risk, contribution/withdrawal timing, calibration, and held-out coverage are validated; publish seed, number of paths, assumptions, confidence interval and limitations. A “78% chance” must never be a hand-picked illustration.

### 5.3 Candidate generation and counterfactuals

Generate actions from *detected portfolio problems*, not a Nifty scan. V1 action grammar: `HOLD` (no trade; any new contribution remains cash), `ADD_CASH_TO_RESERVE` (a designation that lowers deployable cash without changing asset value), `USER_SELECTED_BUY_SCENARIO`, `ADJUST_CONTRIBUTION_SCENARIO`, and `REDUCE_EXPOSURE_PREVIEW` (educational until sale friction/tax lots are known). Candidate universe for the private prototype is current holdings and explicit user-selected scenarios. Add system-generated product candidates only after a reviewed catalogue/policy and legal release gate. For each material issue, include no action and only alternatives with sufficient inputs; explain when no valid alternative exists. Reject unsupported fund/debt products from instrument-level comparison; show category-level scenarios instead. Cap candidate count (e.g. 20) and compute cheap constraints before expensive research.

Evaluation contract: freeze the financial state, valuation, market inputs and policy; validate ownership, identity, price, liquidity, lot size and budget. Apply the **same** external contribution/withdrawal to HOLD and every alternative; HOLD retains new money as cash. Apply each hypothetical transaction, then recompute exposure, stress, goals, risk and coverage from that candidate state. The accounting invariant is: **assets after action + explicit friction = assets before action + external contribution − external withdrawal**. Reject hard violations and unquantifiable sale friction. Report each metric's change relative to the matching HOLD baseline, identify dominance/trade-offs/materiality and return a dated simulation with reason codes. A preferred action may be published only under a reviewed decision policy.

For the **private prototype**, do not pretend an unreviewed weighted score proves one action is best. Show the vector of before/after deltas, hard-gate outcomes, and a Pareto comparison: an action dominates HOLD only if it materially improves at least one measured user-priority dimension, worsens none beyond declared tolerance, and does not rely on missing inputs. Otherwise show the trade-off and leave the choice open; HOLD is the default. Later, after policy review, `utility_delta` can be a transparent *policy choice*: `U = Σ(weight_k × normalized_delta_k) − explicit_cost − uncertainty_penalty`, with logged weights, normalization, policy version and sensitivity. Hard constraints run independently of utility: emergency reserve, affordability, goal horizon, maximum equity/issuer/sector, minimum liquidity, unknown exposure, stale-data tolerance and user restrictions. Hard violations cannot be offset by high expected return. Return forecasts are optional; without one, do not claim return-preserving improvement. Apply minimum absolute rupee and metric changes to prevent churn; if trade-offs are within uncertainty bounds, prefer HOLD/review.

Explanations list: current issue; action amount; before/after table; gates; data coverage; strongest evidence and counterevidence; costs/tax assumptions; why no action and the next best alternative ranked lower; triggers to revisit. LLM may write prose from a validated `DecisionAudit`; numerical fields come from deterministic code. Any generated number not in audit fails validation and suppresses prose. No autonomous trade suggestion from model text.

### 5.4 Thesis and evidence

Create a thesis only for user-owned or seriously considered listed securities. Store user-approved reason, horizon, falsifiable conditions, source/evidence IDs, latest assessment, and review date. Price moves create a “price changed” event, not thesis invalidation. New filings/results can trigger a bounded evidence refresh. `thesis_confidence` is initially a qualitative state (`supported`, `mixed`, `weakened`, `insufficient`) with a change log; do not invent numerical updates like +3/−5 until evidence weight and calibration are tested. Existing research report artifacts and claim ledger supply cited evidence. Contradictions remain visible. A thesis cannot override a portfolio suitability failure.

## 6. Event loop and freshness

Phase 02 adds a typed DB-backed account-sync job because the existing worker only processes legacy recommendations. Phase 07 adds typed decision reviews; Phase 08 adds scheduled **market-close refresh** and issue events, without a separate queue product. Reuse the lease/fencing *pattern*, with new job ownership and result types. Event types: broker import, manual edit, profile/goal/commitment change, quote batch refresh, research evidence change, policy change. Each event carries source ID, occurred/received times, dedup key and affected entity IDs. A dependency map invalidates only affected artifacts:

`account import → state → valuation → exposures → risk/goals → decisions`; `quote batch → valuation → risk/scenarios → decision relevance check`; `goal edit → state/goal projection/decision`; `filing → company thesis → affected decisions`. Quote-on-focus refresh updates a **dated quote overlay**; it does not publish a new decision or increment the financial-state version. A scheduled, bounded valuation snapshot is the input to a review. A daily review checks global portfolio state after affected calculations. Coalesce duplicate quotes; show event recency and “nothing material changed.” Materiality defaults belong in versioned policy and can be tuned only against fixture outcomes. Inbox entries have `urgent/review/information/opportunity/resolved`, fingerprint, first/last seen, state/valuation IDs, dismissal/snooze, and explanation; dedup by issue, not each quote.

Publication follows the existing **fencing pattern**, implemented for the new typed job: compute intermediate artifacts privately, then in one transaction verify worker token/lease and financial-state, valuation and policy versions; insert the immutable outcome, update the current pointer and complete the job. If an input version changed, keep a historical result labelled superseded and enqueue a fresh review. No older review may become current because it finished later. If provider data is partial, preserve the last good state as dated, show sync error and suppress new positive actions.

## 8. Security, suitability, and audit gates

The current fixed-user backend and unauthenticated admin are safe only in a local demo for the owner's account. **Before any public URL, multi-user use, or connection of another person's broker account:** add authenticated app users, CSRF/session protection, user-scoped queries, encrypted broker tokens, safe secret rotation, protected admin, HTTPS, rate limits, deletion/export, and tests for cross-user access. Treat broker/news/research text as untrusted input to any LLM; it cannot call privileged tools or alter numeric audit fields. No order routes or SDK order imports. Decide provider redistribution/licensing before caching or publicly displaying live market data. If the development environment uses a forwarded public URL, disable broker endpoints and show fixtures until app authentication exists. A local setup command must avoid printing session tokens in terminal history or process arguments. The implementation agent should verify the chosen authentication method can satisfy this rule before connecting live.

Indian personalized investment advice is regulated. SEBI's current IA materials require risk profiling/suitability and address record keeping and use/disclosure of AI. Whether this exact product can issue personalized actionable advice needs legal/compliance review before public launch. Until that gate, run in private educational/research mode with proposals clearly marked simulations; wording alone is not a substitute for legal review. ## 12. Detailed implementation specification

This section resolves implementation choices left implicit in the phase map. If existing V3 code already satisfies a contract, reuse it and record the proof; do not create a duplicate service to satisfy a directory sketch.

### 12.1 Source-of-truth matrix

| Fact | Authoritative source in the application | Update trigger | Conflict behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| Broker-held units | Most recent successful import for that **specific** broker account | Authenticated sync | Never blend with older rows from the same account. Keep old import as history. |
| Manual units/value | Latest confirmed manual snapshot for its named account | User confirmation | Manual assets in a separate account survive broker sync. |
| Instrument identity | Verified canonical instrument mapping, preferably ISIN plus exchange/series | Master refresh or explicit correction | Hold unresolved row apart and block instrument-specific action. Symbol text alone does not prove identity. |
| Broker-reported average price/P&L | Broker import, labeled as broker-reported | Broker sync | Keep separate from calculated cost basis and tax lots. A contradictory amount is flagged, not silently corrected. |
| Portfolio valuation | Quote policy selected for a dated portfolio-state version | Price refresh or state rebuild | Prefer declared source order; old state stays immutable. |
| User income, expenses, liabilities and constraints | Latest user-confirmed profile/liability revisions | User edit | Never infer financial capacity or debt terms from broker account balance or market holdings. |
| Goal amount/date/priority | Latest unsuperseded goal version | User edit | Previous decision remains bound to previous goal version. |
| Commitment/SIP | Current create/list rows are unversioned; Phase 04 introduces a user-confirmed revision chain. Broker holdings alone cannot establish a SIP schedule. | User edit/import with explicit mapping | Avoid counting an existing SIP and a proposed SIP twice; until revision exists, do not silently alter an existing row. |
| Product terms/policy | Dated, reviewed catalogue and versioned policy | Approved source update | Synthetic fixtures may run educational scenarios only. |
| Company thesis | User-confirmed thesis version plus cited research artifact | User edit/material evidence review | New evidence creates an assessment revision; it cannot rewrite the user's original reason. |
| Current action | Latest successfully published decision compatible with current financial-state, valuation, profile/goal, policy and freshness requirements | Review publication | Older completed jobs remain history, never silently current. |

Create one identity-resolution function used by Angel, CSV and manual imports. Matching order: validated ISIN and compatible instrument class/exchange → reviewed instrument mapping → user-approved resolution → unresolved. A symbol collision or multiple plausible ISINs yields a preview conflict. Tokens remain in an Angel-specific mapping table; do not put them in the generic instrument key now used for Upstox. A changed broker symbol with unchanged ISIN should preserve the canonical instrument and record the symbol alias. A changed ISIN, merger, split or demerger requires corporate-action handling and cannot be treated as a routine rename.

### 12.2 State construction algorithm

The Portfolio Twin is a frozen *answer to what the system knew at a particular time*, not a mutable cache. Build it transactionally from these stages:

1. Choose the latest **complete, confirmed** snapshot for each included source account at the review cutoff. Explicitly include selected manual/CSV accounts. A disconnected account can remain included from a dated snapshot, but the status is stale/disconnected. A pending partial sync does not displace its previous complete snapshot.
2. Resolve position identity and mark each row `resolved`, `ambiguous`, or `unresolved`; preserve raw label, broker and source-account identity. Never aggregate two rows merely because their display symbols match.
3. Preserve source-observed price/value and their timestamps without choosing a new current valuation yet. A position lacking a source value remains a real position with unknown value.
4. Bind the active profile, risk answer version, goals, commitments and preference restrictions. Record the exact versions. If the profile is absent, the holdings state can exist; suitability decisions cannot.
5. Store every sync attempt and its freshness metadata. Compute a canonical **economic state hash** over ordered normalized positions, included account IDs, user-input version IDs and normalization version; exclude fetch/quote timestamps. If it matches current state, update account freshness but skip a duplicate financial-state publication.
6. Publish a new **financial-state** version with a monotonic per-user number when economic holdings or bound user facts change. Freeze its positions/metadata and mark dependent risk/goals/actions stale. Never mutate it on later provider responses.
7. Select a **separate immutable valuation snapshot** at a cutoff from a declared set of quote/NAV/manual observations and asset-specific price policy. Capture per-position `value`, `price`, `price_time` if supplied, retrieval time, `price_source`, `quality`; if neither price nor user value exists, value remains unknown. Calculate known total, unknown-value count, shares of known value with verified identity/recent price, coverage by each classification dimension and earliest/latest valuation times. If material assets lack values, display “Known value” with the coverage warning rather than a misleading total. Risk, scenarios and decisions bind to both financial-state and valuation IDs. A quote-on-focus UI overlay is labelled with its actual time basis and does not alter the saved decision.

Goal allocations need stable economic identity beyond a legacy `HoldingPosition` row ID. The new claim key is account + resolved canonical asset + source position/product key when the source supplies one; otherwise require a user-confirmed match. On goal edit, copy claims into the new goal revision only after rechecking current ownership and the total available value. On account reimport, rebind only one-to-one matches that still support the claim. If identity is ambiguous, an asset disappears, or value falls below claims, keep historical claims, mark current allocation `needs_review`, reserve uncertain funds conservatively and suppress dependent goal/suitability conclusions. Never carry a claim to a different account just because ticker text matches. The old goal/position IDs remain audit references. All create/edit/rebind routes must check current ownership and current goal version, not only row existence.

Readiness is multidimensional: `account_coverage_status`, `holdings_status`, `valuation_status`, `identity_status`, and `suitability_status` each report `complete`, `partial`, or `unusable` with named missing fields. A complete Angel import does not prove all of the person's accounts are represented; a complete valuation does not imply a complete risk profile. Thresholds for “material” are policy versioned, and unknown-value rows are never assumed to be ₹0. The first UI render shows the last published state/valuation immediately, with a “refreshing” banner if newer work is computing. “No action required” for the person's financial position is allowed only when account coverage and other relevant dimensions plus decision review are complete; otherwise say “No issue found in the assessed holdings” or identify what remains unassessed, without issuing a positive personal action.

### 12.3 Financial schema and migration order

Use additive migrations after current Alembic `0013`; verify actual head first. Do not modify historical migrations. Suggested ownership and constraints:

| Record | Key and uniqueness | Important fields | Retention/revision rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Source account | UUID; unique `(user_id, provider, external_account_fingerprint)` when broker identity exists; locally minted stable UUID for manual/CSV accounts | Masked display name, inclusion choice, status, last success/error, consent time, data categories | Broker fingerprint is keyed HMAC, not a plain hash of a short account code. Soft disconnect; preserve imports until requested deletion. |

Migration sequence: (A) Phase 01 account/import/identity tables and nullable links from existing holdings; (B) Phase 02 typed portfolio-sync job and session metadata; (C) Phase 03 state/position/valuation tables; (D) Phase 04 profile/goal-allocation/commitment revisions; (E) Phases 05–07 risk/scenario/simulation/decision/review-job tables; (F) Phase 08/09 event/inbox/thesis tables only when used. Existing `HoldingsSnapshot` records remain manual legacy input; introduce a compatibility reader instead of backfilling them into broker accounts with guessed account identity. Plan a one-time user-confirmed migration for old manual rows. Any new required column begins nullable with a backfill or only applies to new tables. Test upgrade against a copy of the real dump when Postgres becomes available, verify row counts/checksums before/after, and keep a backup before deploy. Rollback is a route/feature flag to old UI and APIs; destructive downgrades are not an acceptable data recovery strategy.

All money, quantities and prices in financial state use fixed precision Decimal/NUMERIC with documented scale. Store percentages as dimensionless decimal fractions internally (`0.20` means 20%) and name response fields accordingly. Statistical calculations may use float after validating finite input and report a method-specific rounding tolerance. All timestamps have timezone; market display uses Asia/Kolkata with UTC storage. Store trading date and exact exchange timestamp separately because an API retrieval time is not a price time. Keep null, zero and “not applicable” distinct. For a sourced value, keep a provenance tuple: source name, source record/URI, as-of, retrieved-at, transformation version, and quality flag.

The account-fingerprint HMAC key is persistent, locally protected and backed up separately from short-lived Angel session tokens. Losing or rotating it without a controlled rekey would make the same broker account appear new and risk duplicate positions. The operator setup must make this separation explicit; public Phase 10 adds managed secret rotation and account-bound token encryption.

### 12.4 Angel connection operating states

Connection lifecycle: `not_connected → operator_setup → connected → reconnect_required → connected`, with `syncing → synced/partial/failed` tracked separately. Disconnect moves to `disconnected`. A successful login is not a successful holdings sync. A successful HTTP response with `status=false`, a missing `data.holdings`, or a schema mismatch is a failed/partial fetch, never an empty account. A valid **empty** holdings list must be distinguished from a malformed/missing list and confirmed with provider envelope and account identity. `getProfile` client code must match the intended account; derive a keyed fingerprint for account lookup and mask it for display.

Broker API sequence for a sync: confirm server-side session → `getProfile` if identity not already validated → `getAllHolding` → optional `getPosition` and `getRMS` in separate coverage buckets → normalize/validate → preview reconciliation → publish a **complete confirmed** import. The first sync requires an account/overlap preview; later clean syncs may confirm automatically under saved account mapping, while a conflict requires review. Quotes can be fetched after import in batches, but import publication must not depend on quote availability because the broker's holding payload already includes an LTP observation. Historical daily candles are fetched asynchronously only for resolved material holdings whose risk analysis needs them. At every step, log request ID, endpoint name, elapsed time, status category and redacted account hash; do not log request/response bodies or auth headers.

Published rate limits are guidance, not an invitation to run at maximum. Use conservative per-endpoint limits, cache token quotes, and back off on provider throttling (including documented 403 rate-limit failures, not only 429). A provider-wide circuit breaker avoids repeated failures during outage. For expired auth, mark `reconnect_required` and preserve last state; for throttling, queue a retry with `Retry-After` if provided; for network failure, retain last good import. A malformed holding row is quarantined and the batch marked partial with validated rows and source aggregate retained for review, **but that partial batch does not become the account's current complete snapshot**. Users see counts, reconciliation difference and affected identifiers, resolve or explicitly defer them, then confirm a complete batch. Never let a failed or partial sync silently erase previous holdings.

Live market data scope is specific: Angel quotes and candles for token-mapped instruments. Keep RSS, source-backed filings and other providers for news/fundamentals. Use Angel quote for price freshness when permitted; do not replace an adjusted Yahoo candle series with unadjusted Angel candles inside the same return calculation. The history service chooses an explicit series per metric and records corporate-action treatment. If the current SmartAPI app lacks quote or historical access, the holdings import still works and the valuation policy falls back visibly.

### 12.5 Risk assessment policy specification

The system must make three distinct assessments:

* **Tolerance:** behavioral answers and loss comfort, versioned questionnaire. Never infer from realized trades or market exposure alone.
* **Capacity:** emergency reserve coverage, reliable surplus, debt service, near-term spending, dependents, income stability, goal lockup and need to liquidate assets. Use conservative unknown handling: missing essential inputs yields `capacity_unknown` and blocks personalized risk-increasing actions. A qualitative `low/moderate/high` can be derived only from a reviewed policy; until then present raw constraints and a scenario.
* **Requirement:** for each goal, how large the contribution/target gap is under declared low/base/high returns. If required return is implausibly high, say the goal or contribution needs revision; do not raise risk permission to match the gap.

The effective constraint is the strictest of relevant capacity, goal horizon/liquidity, explicit user restriction and reviewed policy, with tolerance an additional ceiling rather than a substitute. Capture *why* each ceiling bound. If there are conflicting goals, earmark assets and budgets; short-horizon goal funds are not available for long-horizon equity proposals. Recompute after a debt/expense/profile change even if broker holdings did not move. A “conservative” label without financial detail is not enough to recommend a debt product: product terms, liquidity and credit/interest-rate risk still need evidence.

Risk fingerprint response for every dimension includes: `value`, `unit`, `direction`, `coverage_fraction`, `method`, `window_start/end`, `source_state_id`, `warnings`, and `status` (`ready`, `estimated`, `insufficient_data`, `unsupported`). For concentration, show issuer and account weights; for funds, unknown look-through remains a bucket. For volatility, require aligned returns and identify whether series is price or total return. Correlation graph edge thickness is sample-correlation strength but the UI must show the sample window and avoid interpreting correlation as causation. Cluster count cannot be called “effective assets” without a defined, validated method; initial UI can say “largest correlated cluster contains X% of known value.”

### 12.6 Scenario computation detail

Each scenario declares a vector of shocks at one consistent horizon, position sensitivity model, missing coverage and date. Supported shocks are applied to **exposure factors**, then mapped once to each instrument return. For example, a pure bank-sector shock changes only verified bank exposure; a broad equity shock changes verified equity exposure. Do not stack two shocks unless a compound scenario explicitly defines residuals to avoid doubling market beta. A bond rate scenario uses `−modified_duration × Δyield` only where duration is known; otherwise mark that position unmodeled. Oil/FX shocks require sourced sensitivity or a user-defined scenario, not sector-name guessing. Cash has zero market-price shock in a simple scenario but may have purchasing-power/currency effects in a different scenario. For an unmodeled slice, give covered value and show both total-account and covered-slice percentages; do not silently dilute loss by counting unknown as flat.

Contribution ledger rows contain `position_id`, source account, pre-shock value, modeled return, modeled value change, reason, evidence/method and quality. Sum of rows equals modeled portfolio change within paise rounding. Scenario UI presents value change, percentage of covered value, largest contributors, what is outside coverage, and one sentence saying scenario is hypothetical. The named templates are versioned; edits to template assumptions create a new version rather than altering old saved runs.

Goal projection order: build month-by-month ledger from current earmarked value, confirmed existing contributions and proposed changes; apply inflation basis exactly once; use explicit nominal/effective return convention; incorporate known fees/loads and flag excluded tax; aggregate terminal values and gap. Keep each goal's allocated starting value and contribution stream separate to prevent the same rupee supporting two goals. A user may compare “increase monthly contribution,” “reduce target,” “extend horizon,” and “change allocation scenario,” but the interface must not imply that riskier assets guarantee the target. Any Monte Carlo output is deferred until calibration gate; do not put a fake probability placeholder in API or UI.

### 12.7 Decision policy, costs and uncertainty

Action catalogue is typed; a buy amount refers to **new money**, existing confirmed cash, or sale proceeds explicitly. “Buy ₹X” without cash source is invalid. A sale preview must identify exact position/account and quantity, never an aggregate ticker that spans accounts or tax lots. If tax lots are unknown, cost/tax becomes an interval/unknown and action status is `review_required`; no net-of-tax advantage is claimed. Fees include brokerage, statutory charges, fund expense/exit load where applicable, spread/slippage assumption and taxes only when calculable. An unknown cost is not zero. Accrued taxes or liabilities are not deducted unless documented.

Independent hard gates run before any action comparison. A sample policy result consists of `(gate_id, pass/fail/unknown, observed_value, allowed_value, data_source, reason)`. `unknown` on a material gate blocks publishing a positive action while still allowing HOLD and a “complete your information” inbox item. A proposal that merely fixes one concentration gate while violating liquidity is rejected. Limit proposed action size to confirmed deployable cash after reserve/earmark obligations, respect unit/lot rounding, and show residual cash. Sales cannot be executed by the app; even an eligible reduce preview is informational pending tax/cost and suitability review.

The first comparison uses goal gap change, worst modeled scenario loss, concentration, liquidity, and explicit cost as a **metric vector**, without a scalar score. Do **not** claim a return delta from a source that only supplies price history. For each candidate, expose raw before/after values and identify dominance or trade-off relative to HOLD. Use uncertainty in three ways: data coverage gate, sensitivity intervals for uncertain metrics, and a minimum difference threshold. A later reviewed policy may add normalized weighted contributions; those weights and sensitivity must then be visible. If differences overlap uncertainty, HOLD/review remains the default rather than “buy because score is 0.01 higher.”

Re-evaluation triggers: material profile/goal change; fresh holdings import; material concentration/liquidity threshold crossing; thesis condition change; materially changed product terms; periodic policy review. A routine daily quote change can update valuation and risk without issuing a new action. A published action has `valid_until` or invalidation conditions; stale advice cannot remain “current” after profile/state mismatch. Never automatically reverse a user-dismissed action without new material evidence. If an action is rejected by user, store explicit reason and expiry and show it in future alternatives.

### 12.8 Research levels and model budget

The user-provided five-level research funnel is a later capability, but the first release uses it as a **cost rule**:

| Level | Inputs and task | Typical use | Stop condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0: Portfolio coverage | Identity, prices, asset type, liquidity and known exposures | Every holding | Unresolved remains unresolved; never summon LLM to guess ISIN. |
| 1: Cheap screen | Current holdings and a small eligible catalogue; basic quality/data availability | Candidate generation | Reject instruments failing hard eligibility/liquidity. |
| 2: Structured research | Existing fundamentals/technicals/news and source dates | Shortlisted securities | Missing critical evidence prevents directional claim. |
| 3: Deep evidence | Filings, earnings call, claim ledger, contradictions, thesis | Material candidate or thesis event | Bound search/time/model calls; produce cited missingness. |
| 4: Forecast/committee challenge | Kronos and other calibrated forecasts, adversarial questions | Explicit deep review only | Omit if calibration unavailable; no role-play vote as independent evidence. |
| 5: Portfolio decision | Pure counterfactual and suitability engine | Finalist action set | Publish only if gates, margin and audit trail pass. |

Existing FinBERT/Kronos/Qwen/Gemini/FinRL/MVO remain optional evidence or research tools. A model call records purpose, input evidence IDs, model/version, prompt version, output hash, cost/latency and failure. Group evidence by underlying data lineage: technical momentum, Kronos and FinRL trained on the same prices do not count as independent confirmations. Confidence display initially reports *data/evidence quality and decision stability*, not an uncalibrated 0–100 conviction score. Forecast ensembles/regime weights require separate walk-forward evaluation with a naive baseline, transaction costs, survivorship controls and point-in-time sources before entering candidate utility.

Every chart has accessible text summary, axis units, date range, source and coverage label. Do not use color alone to communicate risk. Mobile order prioritizes status, action, then details. Motion is optional and must respect reduced-motion settings. Empty, loading, partial, stale, failed and no-action are separate designed states. All tables have meaningful headers and horizontal handling on small screens. Currency is formatted `en-IN` with exact rupee detail in expanded views; percentages specify denominator and round consistently. Avoid “portfolio healthy” unless the policy has a defensible definition; use specific observations instead.

### 12.10 Observability and operations

Track broker sync attempts/success/partial/failure, age of last successful snapshot, unresolved value share, quote coverage and age, state build duration, risk/decision duration, gated-action reason counts, inbox dedup counts, model usage, publication conflicts and user-visible stale states. Metrics must not include holdings details, account IDs, PIN/TOTP or full research documents. Structured logs carry a request/job correlation ID and a hashed account reference. Alerts for repeated auth failures, schema drift, provider throttling (including Angel's documented 403 form), migration mismatch and stuck jobs should indicate affected capability without exposing private data. Backup and restore must include encryption-key handling; a database dump without the key should not leak broker tokens, and a restored system should not silently resume broker sync without reauthorization.

Schedule: sync on explicit user request and one configurable market-close interval while session permits; quote refresh on dashboard focus with cache and a clearly dated quote overlay, labelled exchange-time or retrieval-time according to actual fields; publish a bounded valuation snapshot for scheduled review only after the relevant final close, not a provisional closing-auction reference price. Recompute risk when economic state or saved valuation changes and decision relevance only if material. Do not promise overnight automatic broker sync if Angel sessions expire at midnight and no permitted refresh mechanism is confirmed. On startup, read current published state; schedule missing derived artifacts; do not call the broker in the request path just to render Overview. Apply per-user/provider job dedup and bounded retries. Store result history even when no action is recommended, because “nothing changed” is an auditable decision.

### 12.12 Later roadmap with entry gates

* **Broker expansion (Upstox/Zerodha):** after canonical account import contract and source-coexistence gate A01/A02 are stable. Add one adapter at a time; do not fork domain logic.
* **CAS/statements and OCR:** after real CSV preview and reconciliation are reliable. Use statement-specific parsers, page/row source references and confidence; never let OCR silently overwrite holdings.
* **Mutual-fund look-through:** only with dated scheme holdings and undisclosed remainder; prevent fund-of-fund cycles and double counting. Health and correlation coverage improve accordingly.
* **Tax-lot and transaction ledger:** require transaction/statement source, corporate-action adjustment, transfer matching and realized/unrealized distinction; only then show sale cost/tax estimates.
* **Advanced factor model/correlation graph:** require licensed factor histories, minimum aligned coverage, shrinkage, sample uncertainty and out-of-sample stability; visualize with limits and accessible summary.
* **Probability-based goals:** require calibrated return distribution and sequence-risk model, held-out reliability checks and explicit fee/tax basis; only then add Monte Carlo percentages.
* **Continuous news/event monitoring:** require source-licensing, stable event/entity linking, notification materiality precision and rate budget. Start with scheduled polling, not infrastructure-first streaming.
* **Multi-user/public launch:** complete Phase 10 security/legal gates and data deletion/export. Fixed `SINGLE_USER_ID` must be eliminated, all account/state/action queries scoped by authenticated owner, and broker sessions isolated.
* **Execution/trading:** separate product and regulatory decision. No phase in this plan authorizes it; require user consent, order-risk controls, broker entitlements and audited transaction semantics.

### 12.13 Product-quality evaluation, beyond test coverage

Build a small **golden portfolio review set** before tuning thresholds or explanation wording. Store only synthetic/consented anonymized fixtures, each with expected observations and prohibited conclusions. The purpose is to catch a system that passes arithmetic tests but still gives bad financial guidance. Freeze a held-out subset; do not adjust policy on a case and then count it as independent validation.

| Fixture | Expected answer | Conclusion that must fail |
|---|---|---|
| Five direct banks plus a bank ETF, verified overlap | Banking concentration identified; look-through coverage stated; HOLD or user-selected diversification scenario compared | “Six independent investments” or confident whole-portfolio diversification claim. |
| Broad, well-covered portfolio with sufficient reserve and no goal shortfall | “No material change needed” | Forced buy suggestion merely to populate Actions. |
| High loss tolerance, no reserve, near-term house payment | Capacity/liquidity binds; risk-raising user scenario rejected | Aggressive questionnaire overrides capacity. |
| Cash needed for a dated obligation | Earmarked cash excluded from deployable amount | Same cash funds goal and proposed purchase. |
| Stock falls sharply, no verified thesis event | Price move noted; thesis unchanged/unknown | Automatic SELL or “thesis broken” from price alone. |
| Angel session expired, last import from yesterday | Dated state remains; reconnect item and no new live-data claim | Empty portfolio, fresh status or current action from stale input. |
| Fund holdings unavailable | Fund value counted but look-through/cluster exposure unknown | Full factor/sector precision inferred from scheme name. |
| Proposed sale with unknown acquisition lots | Pre-tax risk scenario possible; tax friction unknown and review required | Precise after-tax improvement or executable sale instruction. |
| Two accounts hold same ISIN | Both positions preserved and consolidated issuer exposure correct | Treat one position as duplicate and delete it. |
| Angel `totalholding` conflicts with rows | Visible reconciliation gap; partial import if material | Invent balancing asset or silently overwrite source values. |

Evaluate three properties separately: **calculation integrity** (all conservation/reconciliation gates), **decision restraint** (zero prohibited conclusions in the golden set; high sensitivity to hard risk/capacity violations), and **explanation fidelity** (every displayed number and causal claim traces to an audit field or cited evidence). Track false urgent issues and user-visible missing-data quality, not click-through or number of recommendations. One person can review the initial fixture set manually; automation should assert the invariant/forbidden claims so future changes do not regress them. If a fixture exposes an unsupported inference, suppress that insight until the data/method gate is satisfied.
