The one rule behind every choice

Deterministic code beats a specialist model, and a specialist model beats a language model. Screening, scoring, allocation and the safety gates are plain Python you can read and test. Models only produce evidence that the code then checks. A language model only writes up evidence it was handed and never supplies a number. And nothing earns influence until it has been tested against what prices actually did next.

Written by hand on purpose

What
The Student t distribution (through the regularised incomplete beta function, Lentz continued fraction), block bootstrap, rank correlation, the money cash-flow ledger, the SSRF-safe fetcher, the hysteresis state machines.
Why
Each is small, central and must be exactly right. A library would add weight without adding trust, and hand-written versions are tested against known values.
You get
No scipy dependency, and statistics whose every step can be read.

Models, and how much each one is trusted

Three kinds of model are present. None of them can change a rupee amount on its own.

Kronos-small

NeoQuasar/Kronos-small, 24.7 M parametersweight 0
What
A foundation model trained on candlestick data. Given up to 400 daily bars it samples future price paths (8 draws, temperature 1.0, top-p 0.9) and the app derives a direction and a 30-day move from them.
Why
A cheap second opinion that costs 30 seconds of CPU per stock, so it runs nightly on a shortlist of about 90 names (watchlist, Nifty 50, one ETF per index), not on all 2,600.
You get
A forecast stored with its inputs every trading night, shown only as a rank against other forecasts made the same day (for example "more upbeat than 94% of 89").
Limit
Tested on random data with zero drift it still returned +4% to +29%, so its absolute number is meaningless and is not displayed. It counts for nothing in any plan until the scoring ledger shows skill. First live results arrive around 18 November.

FinBERT

ProsusAI/finbert, CPUearlier pipeline
What
A language model fine-tuned on financial text that labels a headline positive, neutral or negative.
Why
It is small and runs offline, and a classifier is easier to audit than a chat model.
You get
A news-sentiment evidence input for the earlier stock-candidate pipeline. It does not feed the current review or plan.

Qwen and Gemini (language models)

OpenAI-compatible endpointswrites, never scores
What
Used in two places: the research page (extract claims from fetched text, then write a short report) and the earlier analyst "council" (planner, bull, bear, fundamental, quant, risk, judge).
Why
Reading prose is the one job a language model does better than code. Everything is structured JSON, schema-checked, versioned, and a missing role degrades confidence instead of crashing.
You get
Readable research reports whose claims are verified against the text that was actually read (6 of 6 verified on both test runs).
Limit
The Gemini search tool is out of quota on your key, so source discovery uses Google News RSS and Wikipedia instead. Output is only as good as the headlines it reads.

PyPortfolioOpt mean-variance and the FinRL PPO agent

not driving the plan
What
Mean-variance picks weights from past returns and risk. The PPO agent was trained once on the Nifty 50.
Why
Kept so the earlier candidate-portfolio page works and so the project can say honestly what it tried.
Limit
Plan new investment does not use either for amounts. It does use PyPortfolioOpt's Ledoit-Wolf shrunk covariance, as a description only: volatility and correlations of the proposed stocks versus holdings, with a warning above 0.7. No expected returns, no optimiser. Its allocator is the rule-based one in the Plan maths chapter.

Making the data trustworthy first

A signal built on a wrong price is worse than no signal, so the data is audited before any number is computed.

Split and bonus audit

market/adjust.py
What
For every split or bonus on record, compare the overnight move across the ex-date with the event's factor. Near the factor: the series is still raw, so adjust it. Near 1: Angel already adjusted it, leave it. Small factors (within 15% of 1) cannot be told from a normal move and are reported, never applied. Rights issues and demergers are never adjusted; they mark nearby dates unreliable.
Why
Checked live: Angel's candles were already adjusted (RELIANCE bonus, KOTAKBANK split). Assuming they were raw would have halved prices a second time.
You get
Charts and returns without fake 50% crashes, and a visible list of dates to treat with care.

Total return with dividends

market/total_return.py
What
Adds cash dividends back, reinvested at the ex-date close. Each dividend is multiplied by the product of every later split or bonus factor, because NSE quotes it in the share terms of that day.
Why
Angel's candles are not dividend-adjusted. Measured on 3,669 real dividend events: the ex-day price drop is 0.6 to 1.0 of the dividend, so ignoring dividends would understate returns. Without the scaling step 533 events were overstated about twofold.
You get
Honest return figures: 3,755 dividends applied, and any dividend it cannot scale with certainty is skipped and counted, never guessed.

Identity: ISIN is the spine

What
Every holding is matched by ISIN, checked with the ISO 6166 Luhn check digit. A symbol alone never proves identity. An ETF has two catalogue rows (NSE listing and AMFI's fund row); the traded listing wins, and only a real tie is called ambiguous.
Why
Symbols are reused and renamed; an ISIN is stable. Guessing would silently attach the wrong price to your money.
You get
Correct prices and sectors for 2,593 stocks, 351 ETFs and 8,658 funds, and an explicit "unmatched" when it cannot be sure.

Expense-ratio matching, and fund NAV trust rules

What
AMFI's expense-ratio file is joined to funds only when the fund house matches and the normalised name matches as a set of words. A ratio of 0 means "not applicable", above 4% is a data error, and Direct above Regular is contradictory: all become unknown. NAV history from mfapi is stored only if it agrees with AMFI (same date, or the same ISIN and within 5% on a date at most 4 days earlier).
Why
A wrong cost on a fund is a wrong recommendation. A false match is worse than a blank.
You get
8,348 funds and ETFs with a defensible cost, and cost-aware ETF choice (for example TATAGOLD over GOLDBEES).

Signals: describing the past, and what changed

All thresholds are named placeholders in the code (signals-policy-p0-unreviewed). Nothing here is a forecast.

SignalMathsWhy, and what you get
Trendclose / 200-day average - 1Whether a price sits above or below its long-run level. Needs 253 closes.
Momentum 12-1close 21 sessions ago / close 252 sessions ago - 1Skips the last month, the standard way to avoid short-term reversal. Highly correlated with trend, so the two are one vote, never two.

"What changed in what you hold" uses hysteresis, with margins measured on 587 stocks

What
State machines replayed over the stored history. A plain crossing of the 200-day average flips a median 7.5 times a year, which is noise. So: trend needs a 3% margin and 3 closes (median 1.7 changes a year); a drawdown enters at -20% and clears above -15% (median 1.3 a year); a deep one enters at -30%, clears above -25%; volatility rising enters when the 60-day is 1.5 times the 252-day and clears below 1.3.
Why
An alert that fires constantly gets ignored. The margins were chosen by measuring real flip rates, and the plain-rule count is shown beside the rule's own.
You get
A short list of real changes, each with its date, how often the plain rule would have cried wolf, and what you can do, starting with "keep things as they are".

The stock ranking: value, quality, momentum

scoring/ranking.py, scoring/momentum.pyunvalidated, saved daily
What
Three separate component scores, each the average of percentile ranks (0 to 100) of its measures within sector peers. Value: earnings yield (EPS over price), EBITDA over EV, book yield. Quality: ROE, margins, growth, low debt, dividend payout (trailing dividends over EPS, capped at 100%). Momentum: 12-1 and 6-1 on total return, counted as one vote. Percentiles are winsorised at 5 and 95, and small sectors are shrunk toward the whole universe by n/(n+4). Banks and insurers are ranked only against each other and never on debt or EV. The composite is an equal-weight mean (weights are configurable and printed). A stock missing a component is not ranked; nothing is zero-filled. Risk and data coverage are shown beside the rank, never inside it.
Why
Value, quality and momentum are the three characteristics with the longest published record in Indian stocks. Ranks within peers avoid hand-set cut-offs, and keeping risk apart stops a volatile stock hiding behind a good score.
You get
Plan stock picks: composite at or above 60, at most 2 per sector, no sector forced, at most 3% each, with the strongest and weakest measures, coverage and dates printed per name. A cross-check drops market-cap based measures for names whose figures disagree (INFY, HCLTECH, INDIGO were flagged) and names the reason.
Limit
Fundamentals are Yahoo-backed and Nifty 50 only. A weekly job now refreshes them, adds one fiscal year of statements (total assets, operating cash flow, capex, net income) so accruals, cash profitability and free-cash-flow yield are active, and cross-checks trailing EPS against the sum of NSE's last four quarterly filings. A disagreement is a flag, never a correction. Only momentum could be back-tested (about +1.4% a month net versus the universe, interval includes zero). Value and quality are untested, and the composite is registered as live claim L9.

The earlier checklist score

scoring/checklist.pyinformation only
What
The first stock score: valuation 30, quality 35, debt 15, risk 20, with risk mixed into the number. Still computed and saved daily (claim L8) but no longer picks plan stocks.
Why kept
So the old and new methods can both be tested against later prices.

The allocator

allocation/plan.py
What
1. Targets in rupees from the band. 2. Buy-only fill: close the most underweight bucket first, then share what is left by target weight. 3. Buckets too small to clear the ₹1,000 minimum are merged into the bucket with the largest shortfall. 4. Whole units only, rounding down; leftover rupees are spent one unit at a time on the leg furthest below its budget, never past a stock's 3% cap. 5. Drift is flagged only beyond 5 percentage points. 6. Money claimed for goals due within 3 years stays in the safe bucket first.
Why
It never sells, because without your purchase lots the tax on a sale cannot be estimated. A buy-only fill fixes imbalance using new money alone.
You get
A concrete list of units and rupees that balances to the paisa (money in equals holdings plus 0.3% assumed charges).

ETF choice: liquidity guard, then lowest cost

What
Among ETFs tracking the same index, only those trading at least 20% as much as the most traded are candidates; the lowest known expense ratio wins, an unknown cost never beats a known one, and with no known costs volume decides.
Why
Cost compounds every year, but a cheap ETF you cannot trade is a trap.
You get
For example NIFTYBEES at 0.04% a year, with the reason printed.

The safety gates every purchase must pass

decisions/engine.py
What
14 checks: data fresh, price known, lot size, minimum action ₹1,000, cost under 1% of the trade, funding, cash and budget available, money not claimed for goals, your restrictions, risk capacity, tax lots known, sale within holding, and concentration limits (one company at most 25%, one sector at most 40%, at least 80% of value freshly priced). A plan is compared against simply holding. For a buy from cash the comparison is always "worse than hold" by the metrics, so a plan's status rests on the hard gates alone.
Why
The same gates serve the action lab and the allocator, so the two cannot disagree. The system is allowed, and expected, to say "no".
You get
Ready, needs your input (with exactly what is missing), or blocked, plus a purchase-by-purchase table of every check.

Small amounts

What
Below ₹1,003 no leg is produced. Instead: a liquid fund and a Nifty 50 index fund (bought by rupee amount, SIPs from about ₹100 in Angel, minimums differ by fund) and any ETF units that fit.
Why
Fixed charges swamp a ₹250 order, and a share cannot be split.
You get
Something useful at any size, and an honest reason stocks are skipped.

Risk and stress

risk/exposure.py, stress.py, volatility.py
What
Asset mix; issuer concentration with the Herfindahl index (sum of squared weights) and its inverse, "effective number of holdings"; sector weights from the instrument list or the market catalogue; months of expenses in cash. Portfolio volatility needs 252 aligned returns covering at least 80% of value, else it shows nothing. Stress scenarios shock equity one-for-one (beta 1): market -20%, Financial Services -20%, IT -25%.
Why
Concentration and cash cushion are the risks a beginner most often misses, and each figure says how much of your money it actually covers.
You get
A per-holding table of what a fall would do, and an explicit "outside the scenario" amount for what it cannot model (funds, gold).
Limit
Correlation between holdings is marked unsupported rather than faked.

Your finances, and how they limit the plan

Risk tolerance

behavioural answers only
What
Three questions score 0 to 100 each and are averaged. If a 20% fall: sell all 0, sell some 25, hold 60, buy more 100. Priority: protect capital 0, balanced 50, maximum growth 100. Largest fall you could accept: 0-10% 0, 10-20% 25, 20-30% 50, 30-50% 75, over 50% 100. Below 34 is conservative, below 67 moderate, otherwise aggressive.
Why
How you behave in a fall decides whether a plan survives the first bad month.
You get
One band that picks the mix. It is a ceiling, never a substitute for capacity.

Capacity and constraints

What
Essential outgo = expenses + recorded loan EMIs. Reserve coverage = reserve / outgo, compared with your target months. Also surplus after essentials and loan payments over income. The effective limit is the strictest of capacity, goal horizon (goals due within 3 years), your exclusions and tolerance. Anything unknown restricts added risk.
Why
What you can afford to lose is a different question from what you feel comfortable losing.
You get
Plain reasons a plan is blocked ("reserve covers 2 months; target is 6").

Goals

goals/projection.py, services/cash_flow_engine.py
What
A month-by-month Decimal ledger where every month satisfies balance after = balance before + contribution + growth - fees. Low, base and high scenarios at 4%, 8% and 11% a year. A search (0.1% steps up to 100%) finds the return you would need, and a back-solve finds the monthly amount needed. Money can be stated in today's rupees with an inflation assumption.
Why
A goal outlook must be reproducible and must not hide an assumption.
You get
"On track under the base assumption" or "needs about 62.9% a year": an illustration, never a probability.

Does outside research support these choices?

Checked 2 October against published work. Supports the direction; none of it proves this particular score works.

Value, quality, momentum in India

supported, with limits
Found
1993 to 2013: momentum earned about 21% a year, value about 6%, size about nothing (Agarwalla, Jacob and Varma). A quality-minus-junk factor earned about 0.92% a month of alpha, and a long-only quality portfolio 0.69%, driven by profitability and payout, and it works within large caps (IIMA working paper, 2022).
So
All three components have an Indian record. Value is the weakest and was poor in 2011 to 2020, so equal weights are not backed by evidence that value deserves a third. Payout is a driver of quality there, so it was added to our quality score (method version p2).

Momentum can crash

risk to know
Found
Momentum has rare, deep losses, mostly when the market rebounds after a fall (Daniel and Moskowitz). One blog back-test of the NSE momentum index finds the gain sits in less-liquid names (backtestindia, not peer reviewed). Our own test saw the same pattern.
So
Nifty 50 is the most liquid slice, so expect a weaker momentum effect than headline numbers.

Why index funds stay the core

supported
Found
Over five years 92.9% of Indian large-cap funds trailed their benchmark in the mid-2023 SPIVA India scorecard.
So
Stocks stay a capped satellite (3% each) and index ETFs stay the base. Equal-weight legs follow DeMiguel, Garlappi and Uppal (2009): optimisers often fail to beat 1/N once estimation error counts, as discussed in Ledoit and Wolf. Their shrinkage is used here only to describe risk.

Accruals

inactive, fine
Found
The accruals effect has faded in the US since about 2002 (Green, Hand and Soliman).
So
Leaving it switched off for lack of inputs costs little.

The evidence machinery: does any of it work?

This is the part most apps skip. It exists so that a signal gains influence only by proving itself.

Rank IC, one observation per date

What
On each month-end, rank stocks by a feature and correlate that rank (Spearman) with the next 21 sessions' return. The unit of evidence is a date, not a stock, because stocks on the same day share the market's move.
Why
Treating 580 stocks as independent would overstate the evidence enormously.
You get
A fair answer to "did higher-ranked stocks do better?"

Honest statistics

What
Student t interval through a hand-written incomplete beta function; Bonferroni correction (alpha 0.05 divided by the number of claims, now 8); a 5,000-resample bootstrap of per-date results with a fixed seed (blocks of consecutive dates are supported); the minimum detectable effect at 80% power; a verdict ladder (no data, too early, underpowered, no evidence, negative, suggestive in the past, promising, earned).
Why
Test many ideas and one will look good by luck. The correction and the "cannot tell" verdict stop that.
You get
Findings such as "the study could not have seen an effect smaller than 0.068", instead of false confidence.

Frozen designs, and two kinds of evidence

What
Each study design is hashed before any result exists. The backtest (45 month-ends, about 580 stocks, five years) can never reach "earned", because it is survivor-biased, price-based and partly in-sample. Live evidence is the only kind that can: each signal and score is stored when made, entered at the next close, scored 21 sessions later, and needs 24 separate dates (about two years). The live registry is version 4 with 8 claims (trend, momentum, calm stocks, volatility persistence, forecast, trend breaks held, checklist score, stock ranking) plus one plan-accounting line.
Why
You cannot move the goalposts after seeing results.
You get
A scorecard that says what is proven: so far, none of the return signals, and only volatility persistence in the past data.

Engineering that protects your data

Insert-only, versioned facts

What
Signals, forecasts, scores, suggestions, plans and outcomes are inserted once and never edited. A method change is a new version.
You get
Anything can be checked against what happened later.

Job queue without Celery

What
A Postgres table polled by the worker, claimed with SELECT ... FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED, a 300-second lease, a fence token so a stale worker cannot overwrite a newer result, bounded retries.
You get
No Redis to run, and a crash never double-processes.

Idempotent by hashing

What
The same inputs give the same stored record: portfolio state hash, plan inputs hash, import content hash and idempotency keys.
You get
Pressing a button twice or retrying a failed request never duplicates anything.

Read-only broker client

What
Hand-written, no Angel SDK, one allowed host, a fixed list of calls (login, profile, holdings, positions, funds, quote, candles). Your client code is stored only as a keyed HMAC fingerprint; the PIN and TOTP are typed in your terminal and never stored.
You get
The app cannot place, change or cancel an order, and a leak of the database would not reveal your account.

Local-only by construction

What
Everything binds to 127.0.0.1; cross-site writes are rejected by an origin check; the fetcher refuses private addresses.
You get
Nothing on your network can reach your portfolio.

Money is Decimal

What
Every rupee amount is exact decimal, rounded to the paisa at defined points, with conservation checks (money in equals holdings plus charges).
You get
Totals that balance exactly.

Research: from a question to a verified report

The pipeline

What
Discover sources (Gemini search if it has quota, else Google News RSS and Wikipedia) → fetch safely (resolve DNS, require a public address, pin that address, follow redirects by hand, cap the bytes; HTML, text and RSS only) → extract short passages → claims pulled by the language model → verify each claim against the passage it came from (supported, unsupported, unknown, contradicted) → write the report only if the evidence verifies, with named gaps.
Why
A model that can browse can also be fooled by a page. Fetched text is treated as data, never as instructions.
You get
A short report with supporting and opposing evidence, each item with publisher and date, and a list of what is missing.
Limit
Headlines are not filings, and PDF text extraction is not installed, so annual reports cannot be read yet.
